The Gap

No Pacific island country has a functioning Aid Information Management System. Development Gateway's AMP has been deployed in 25+ countries but never reached the Pacific. Finance ministries with single-digit staff manage relationships with dozens of development partners using spreadsheets, email, and memory.

The IATI Strategic Plan 2026–2030 prioritises embedding aid data in country-level systems (Action Area A). The Development Intelligence Lab recommends "creating a donor mapping system" for Pacific donor coordination. These prototypes demonstrate that the data and technology already exist — built from IATI, DFAT, NZ MFAT, and SPC data that updates every 12 hours.
