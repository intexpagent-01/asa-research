What drives the movement

Two forces move these numbers. First, real disbursements: a new grant, a tranche payment, or the end of a contract. Second, the publication cycle: IATI data arrives in batches, not continuously. When a funder publishes a batch of transactions, countries jump. When the batch ages out of the 90-day window and the funder has not published another, countries fall — even if spending continues. The data freshness page shows which funders are current and which are not.

Australia’s IATI data ends 30 June 2025. As that date recedes further beyond the 90-day window, countries where Australia is the largest funder will increasingly understate their actual aid picture. This is not a bug in the Signal — it is the fact the Signal exists to make visible.
