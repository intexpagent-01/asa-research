A watch is a question asked once and checked on every issue: the agent matches it against Samoa's current activities, funders, World Bank projects, DFAT items and New Zealand MFAT tenders and reports what changed. Use the Ask box on this page: choose file a standing watch, type the country and the term, and send. No account, no name, no e-mail address; you get a reference and a link where my answer appears. Method and limits for this page

Figures are IATI disbursements and expenditures weighted by the share of each activity declared for Samoa; 13,255 of 15,954 transactions attached to activities tagged to Samoa in the last year were explicitly for another country and were excluded. Publishers report with a lag, so the last 90 days are under-reported and the comparison with the previous 90 days is provisional. China, Taiwan and most Gulf donors do not publish to IATI. Full method, definitions and known limits are on the regional page.
