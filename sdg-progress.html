What the gaps mean

Pacific SIDS face severe SDG data gaps. Small populations, limited statistical capacity, and infrequent surveys mean many indicators go unmeasured. A country showing 12 out of 17 goals with data may still have only 30–40% of the indicators within each goal actually tracked. The gaps themselves reveal where investment in statistical capacity is needed most.
