Positioning Recommendations

For a challenger

  1. Lead with the scope gap. ASI ran an MSD program. Strongim Ekonomi requires governance, PFM, revenue collection, audit, customs — capabilities the evaluation does not evidence. The evaluation found “mixed success” in government collaboration; the new program makes this the central mandate.
  2. Use the evaluation’s own words on transparency. “Several stakeholders were confused or critical about processes for partner selection.” For a program now managing SIG technical advisers and budget support MEL, this is disqualifying context without demonstrated improvement.
  3. Propose an integrated governance model. Show how economic growth and PFM reinforce each other: revenue reform enables public investment; private sector growth broadens the tax base. This is the logic of consolidating the programs, and the winning bidder must demonstrate they understand it.
  4. Offer portfolio-level MEL credibility. The MC must evaluate budget support, twinning arrangements, and infrastructure alongside economic growth. This requires institutional assessment capability, not MSD monitoring.
  5. Address disability inclusion. Zero PWD jobs in 9 years of Strongim Bisnis. A concrete disability employment strategy would differentiate immediately.
  6. Leverage the labour migration opportunity. The evaluation flagged it. The Development Partnership Plan emphasises it. Connecting returning seasonal workers’ skills and remittances to business development is an unaddressed opportunity.

For the incumbent (ASI)

  1. Address the governance gap head-on. Partner with or recruit PFM/governance specialists. Show how 9 years of SIG relationships enables governance reform that a new entrant would need years to build.
  2. Demonstrate the transparency improvements since 2023. If Phase 3 implemented quarterly SIG updates and clearer partner selection criteria, evidence them.
  3. Emphasise transition risk. 44 active partnerships, 30+ local staff, established ministry relationships, business community trust. A new MC would need 12+ months to rebuild, with continuity costs in a fragile context.
  4. Show portfolio-level thinking. Demonstrate understanding of how Strongim Bisnis, ASIP-Gov, SIIP, budget support, and PHAMA Plus interact. DFAT wants the MC who understands the whole picture.
  5. Propose a VfM framework now. The evaluation said none existed. Present what Phase 3 built, or propose one covering the expanded scope. At AUD 17 per dollar of income generated, the cost-effectiveness argument needs strengthening.
