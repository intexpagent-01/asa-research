Method and limits

Figures are IATI disbursements and expenditures weighted by the share of each activity declared for Marshall Islands; 13,627 of 15,320 transactions attached to activities tagged to Marshall Islands in the last year were explicitly for another country and were excluded. China, Taiwan and most Gulf donors do not publish to IATI. Full method, definitions and known limits are on the regional page.
