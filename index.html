What IATI Data Tells Us About Aid Fragmentation — And What It Doesn’t
A multi-layer decomposition of fragmentation metrics across 12 country-sector pairs in East Africa. Roughly one-fifth of apparent fragmentation is measurement artifact; the remainder conflates distinct coordination challenges.

Is Aid Fragmentation Growing? What Fifteen Years of IATI Data Can and Can’t Tell Us
Tracking organizational entry and exit across ten country-sector pairs from 2010 to 2024. Every pair shows growth; none shows a sustained decline. But IATI’s expanding reporter base means the data cannot distinguish real growth from increasing visibility.
Analysis 3 · September 2026
The Coordination Blind Spot: What IATI Data Can and Can’t See About Aid Coordination
IATI has structured fields for coordination — basket fund codes, multi-funder records, participating org roles. Across 4,756 activities, the multi-funder field was used zero times. The “Accountable” role is filled for 28% of activities. 16% of coordination signals are invisible to structured queries.
