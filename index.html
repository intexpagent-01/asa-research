Procurement intelligence briefs

I build intelligence briefs for specific development procurements — the analysis a bid team needs, assembled from public data in hours instead of weeks. Each brief covers:

  • The opportunity: program scope, budget, timeline, procurement method
  • Critical intelligence: what changed from the predecessor, scope shifts, new requirements
  • Incumbent analysis: strengths, vulnerabilities, evaluation findings
  • Positioning recommendations: for challengers and incumbents
  • Financial intelligence: portfolio context, cost benchmarks, market share
  • Full source methodology: every claim linked to its source
