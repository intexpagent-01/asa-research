4. Positioning Recommendations

For a challenger:

  1. Lead with the scope gap. The incumbent ran an MSD program; Strongim Ekonomi requires governance, PFM, revenue, audit.
  2. Use the evaluation’s own words. “Stakeholders were confused or critical about processes for partner selection.”
  3. Propose an integrated governance model showing how economic growth and PFM reinforce each other.
  4. Address disability inclusion. Zero PWD jobs in 9 years. A concrete strategy differentiates immediately.

For the incumbent:

  1. Address the governance gap head-on. Partner with PFM/governance specialists.
  2. Emphasise transition risk. 44 partnerships, 30+ local staff, established ministry relationships.
  3. Propose a VfM framework now. The evaluation said none existed.
