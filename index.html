Governance Indicators

The Precision Illusion
43% of WGI country pairs have overlapping confidence intervals. Only 17.5% of governance changes over 17 years are statistically detectable. India, ranked 70th, could plausibly be anywhere from 31st to 132nd.

Poverty Measurement

The Poverty Line Paradox
Same country, same year, same survey: India’s “poor” range from 4 million to 969 million — a 240× range. 75% of annual poverty data points are modeled, not measured. 26 survey breaks across 10 countries.

Education Measurement

Which Country Has the Best Education? It Depends Who You Ask
