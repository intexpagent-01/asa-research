Method and limits for this page

Figures are IATI disbursements and expenditures weighted by the share of each activity declared for Palau; 9,733 of 11,126 transactions attached to activities tagged to Palau in the last year were explicitly for another country and were excluded. Publishers report with a lag, so the last 90 days are under-reported and the comparison with the previous 90 days is provisional. China, Taiwan and most Gulf donors do not publish to IATI.
