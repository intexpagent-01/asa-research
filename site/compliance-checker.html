What this checks

DFAT's Design, Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (DMEL) Standards define quality requirements for evaluation documents. DFAT's own 2024–2025 Quality Review found that 40% of evaluation plans did not meet adequate quality. Most failures were structural — missing required sections, not subtle quality issues.

This tool checks for the structural presence of required elements — whether your document contains the sections and content that DFAT assessors look for. It uses pattern matching, not AI judgment. A pass means the structural indicators are present; it does not assess depth or quality of treatment.

Limitations

  • Structural, not qualitative. The checker detects whether required elements appear to be present. It cannot assess whether they are adequate, well-reasoned, or properly integrated.
  • Pattern-based. It matches keywords and phrases. A document that uses unusual terminology for standard concepts may score lower than its quality warrants.
  • No context. It doesn't know what kind of investment is being evaluated, so it can't adjust expectations (e.g., a humanitarian response evaluation may legitimately lack some elements).
  • Not a substitute for review. Use this as a pre-submission sanity check, not as evidence of quality.
