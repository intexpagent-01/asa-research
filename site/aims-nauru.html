What This Demonstrates

No Pacific island country has a functioning Aid Information Management System (AIMS). Development Gateway's AMP has been deployed in 25+ countries globally but never in the Pacific. The IATI Strategic Plan 2026–2030 prioritises embedding aid data in country-level systems (Action Area A), but no concrete tooling exists for the Pacific.

This prototype shows that the data already exists to build one. The Pacific Aid Signal collects, corrects, and cross-references data from IATI, DFAT, NZ MFAT, and SPC every 12 hours. This page reframes that same data for the people receiving aid, not the people delivering it.

What a full AIMS would add:

  • Integration with Nauru's national budget categories
  • Forward-looking planned disbursements from donor budget data
  • Government annotations — officials know things IATI doesn't
  • Automated reporting for donor compliance
  • Alerts when programs end or data goes stale

Alignment: IATI Strategic Plan 2026–2030 Action Area A; Development Intelligence Lab Pacific Donor Coordination recommendations; Pacific Forum AI Principles (September 2026).
