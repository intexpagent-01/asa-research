# SDG Relevance — Pacific Aid Signal

How the Pacific Aid Signal contributes to the Sustainable Development Goals.

## Primary SDG alignment

### SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals

The Signal's core function is making development finance data accessible, timely, and accurate for coordination.

| Target | Contribution |
|--------|-------------|
| 17.2 — ODA commitments | Tracks actual disbursements against 14 Pacific countries from 40+ funders. Identifies gaps between commitments and flows (e.g., DFAT data 15+ months stale while being the largest funder in 9/14 countries). |
| 17.3 — Mobilize additional financial resources | Aggregates financial flows across bilateral, multilateral, and regional sources. Country pages show the full funder landscape, not just one donor's view. |
| 17.15 — Respect country policy space and leadership | AIMS dashboards (14 countries) present aid data from the recipient government's perspective — what a planning officer needs to coordinate partners and align aid with national priorities. |
| 17.16 — Multi-stakeholder partnerships | Cross-publisher deduplication resolves $388M in double-counted aid. Identifies which funders overlap in which sectors and countries. |
| 17.19 — Capacity-building to increase availability of data | Data quality corrections (71% of Pacific-tagged IATI transactions are for other countries), data currency tracking (per-funder staleness), and automated gap alerts build the evidence base Pacific governments need. |

### SDG 16 — Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

| Target | Contribution |
|--------|-------------|
| 16.6 — Effective, accountable, transparent institutions | Makes aid flows visible to citizens and governments. Published openly with methodology documentation. Tracks procurement pipeline and tender outcomes. |
| 16.7 — Responsive, inclusive, participatory decision-making | Standing watch system lets anyone monitor specific aid topics. All data freely accessible via CSV exports, MCP server, and web interface. |

### SDG 1 — No Poverty

| Target | Contribution |
|--------|-------------|
| 1.a — Mobilization of resources for poverty eradication | Tracks where development resources flow across Pacific island states, many of which have the highest aid-to-GNI ratios globally. Identifies concentration risks and funding gaps. |
| 1.b — Pro-poor policy frameworks | Lessons Engine synthesises evaluation evidence from 3,043 World Bank project outcomes across 176 countries, enabling evidence-informed policy design. |

## Secondary SDG connections

The Signal monitors aid activities across all development sectors. While it does not directly deliver outcomes in these areas, it improves the information environment for planning and coordination:

| SDG | Connection |
|-----|-----------|
| SDG 4 — Quality Education | Tracks education sector aid flows and activities (e.g., DFAT-1091 PHC4PNG, Kiribati Education). Links to evaluation evidence. |
| SDG 5 — Gender Equality | Monitors gender-tagged aid (e.g., PWLES — Pacific Women Lead, $62.8M). Sector analysis shows gender-related spending distribution. |
| SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation | Tracks WASH sector activities. Design Brief tool synthesises WASH evaluation lessons across 52 countries. |
| SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy | Monitors renewable energy disbursements ($12.8M in 90-day Pacific data). Design Brief tool covers energy sector evidence. |
| SDG 13 — Climate Action | Tracks climate-relevant activities (1,802 activities, $3.0B lifetime across 14 Pacific countries per IATI classification). |
| SDG 14 — Life Below Water | Monitors ocean and fisheries activities relevant to Pacific SIDS. |

## How this is used

The SDG mapping describes how the tool's functions connect to the Goals framework. The Signal does not claim to achieve SDG targets — it provides data infrastructure that supports the actors working toward them. The primary value is in SDG 17 (data availability, transparency, and coordination for development partnerships), with secondary benefits across sector-specific Goals through improved information for planning.

## Data sources for this mapping

- IATI activity sector codes (DAC 5-digit) mapped to SDGs
- IATI activity SDG tag fields where publishers include them
- World Bank project sector and theme codes
- Disbursement amounts weighted by recipient-country share
